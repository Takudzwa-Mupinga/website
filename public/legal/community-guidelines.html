Community Guidelines

Effective date: 4 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 4 October 2026

Designlynk works because Clients and Professionals trust each other. These guidelines set out what that looks like in practice. They're written in plain language on purpose — if something here ever conflicts with the formal Terms and Conditions, the Terms and Conditions apply, but this is the version we actually want people to read.

1. Be honest about who you are

Use your real name and real qualifications. If you say you can do something, make sure you can. Clients are trusting your profile to make a hiring decision — an inflated CV doesn't just hurt them, it hurts the trust the whole Platform depends on.

2. Be professional and respectful

Treat every Client and Professional the way you'd want to be treated on a job site. No harassment, threats, discrimination, or abusive language — in messages, reviews, or anywhere else on the Platform.

3. Deliver what you promise

Set a realistic timeline, and communicate early if something's going to slip rather than going silent. Clients: give clear briefs and timely feedback — Professionals can't hit a moving target.

4. Keep the job on Designlynk

If you meet someone here, keep the work and the payment here too, for at least 12 months from when you were introduced. It's how we keep the lights on, and it's what keeps escrow protection and dispute support available to both of you.

5. Respect intellectual property and confidentiality

Don't share a Client's files, drawings, or project details outside the Engagement without permission. Don't use a Client's confidential information for anything other than the job you were hired to do.

6. Leave honest reviews

Rate and review based on what actually happened. Don't trade reviews, don't write fake ones, and don't pressure someone into a good rating. We'll remove reviews that are clearly fraudulent or abusive, but we won't remove a fair review just because someone doesn't like it.

7. No fraud or scams

Don't create fake accounts, misrepresent an Engagement, or try to get someone to pay or be paid outside the Platform's safeguards.

8. If something goes wrong

Report it. Use the in-app reporting tools or contact support@designlynk.co.za. We'd rather hear about a problem early than after it's escalated.

9. What happens if these guidelines are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may reach out for clarification, issue a warning, limit account features, or suspend or remove an account. This is a commercial account decision under our Terms and Conditions, not a disciplinary process — but it's one we take seriously, because the whole Platform depends on people being able to trust each other here.

These guidelines work alongside the Designlynk Terms and Conditions, which remain the binding legal document.
